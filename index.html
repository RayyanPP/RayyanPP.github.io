01 Profile

Researcher in statistics and sports analytics

Background, research focus and the methods I work with.

I am a PhD researcher in Mathematics and Statistics at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, combining industry data science experience with rigorous statistical research. My doctoral thesis, supervised by Dr. Llorenç Badiella Busquets and Dr. Pere Puig Casado, studies the impact of yellow cards in soccer across team performance, player behaviour, refereeing, injury risk and player economics.

I build and evaluate predictive models in Python and R, from XGBoost and neural-network ensembles to Bayesian networks, GLMMs and survival models. I previously worked as a Data Scientist at Mercor, completed a BIOSTATNET-funded research stay at the University of Valencia, and taught undergraduate inferential statistics for over a year.

I am open to research collaborations and to roles in sports analytics with clubs, data teams and research groups.

03 Research

Ongoing research

Doctoral research, manuscripts under review and funded projects.

Doctoral thesis In progress

Impact of yellow cards in soccer: A multi-domain analysis

A five-domain study of what happens after a booking, combining GLMMs, interrupted time series, survival models, panel-data methods and machine learning (XGBoost, LSTM, ANFIS).

Supervisors
Dr. Llorenç Badiella Busquets
Dr. Pere Puig Casado
Timeline
Oct 2025 – Sep 2029 (expected)
  • Team performance Possession, xG, pass accuracy and momentum
  • Player behaviour Fouling, tackling and risk-avoidance after a booking
  • Referee decisions Crowd-pressure and fatigue-related biases
  • Injury risk Short-term risk after disciplinary events
  • Economic impact Salaries, contract renewals and transfer value
Thesis paper In progress

Disciplinary escalation in elite soccer: yellow card accumulation and subsequent red card risk

Models red cards as the response, using interval-level match data from Europe's top five leagues, to measure how accumulated yellow cards change the risk of a later red card.

Part of my PhD thesis · UAB · 2026

Journal article Under review

Valuing second-hand cars in India with machine learning: rigorous evaluation, calibrated price intervals and market evidence

Compares Random Forest, XGBoost, Lasso, gradient boosting, KNN and ANN models for the Indian used-car market. A hybrid XGBoost + ANN ensemble performed best (RMSE 1.75, MAE 1.08) and is deployed as a Streamlit web app.
